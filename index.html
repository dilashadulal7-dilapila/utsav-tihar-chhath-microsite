The festival where everyone gets a turn

Tihar is Nepal’s festival of lights. For five days it honours crows, dogs, cows, oxen and finally brothers, so every being that keeps a home alive gets a tika, a garland and a lamp.

Only days later, along the rivers of the Terai, families stand waist-deep in water and offer thanks to the sun. Fire lights the first festival. The sun closes the second.

Discover Chhath

Chhath honours Surya, the sun, and Chhathi Maiya. Families, especially across the Terai, stand in rivers and ponds to give thanks for life, health and harvest. It lasts four days.

  1. Nahay KhayBathing, cleaning the home and eating one pure meal.
  2. KharnaA day-long fast, broken at dusk with kheer and roti.
  3. Sandhya ArghyaThe offering to the setting sun at the ghat.
  4. Usha ArghyaThe offering to the rising sun, then the fast ends.
